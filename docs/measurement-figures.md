# Measurement figures

Home of every measured number, static count, and refutation recorded for spoint. `AGENTS.md` keeps the rules that govern how measurements are taken; this file keeps the figures, their instruments, and what replaced what.

Tags on every entry (`[W:unspecified]` means the source records no commit; `[STRUCK]` means the number is no longer quoted, and the section names its replacement; `[AWAITING-RE-DERIVATION]` means the figure is kept but waits for a re-derivation on a fixed instrument):

- `[STAT:...]` the statistic that produced the number. `static` and `count` are code-derived, not timed. Per-pass percentiles and per-pass averages are INVALIDATED; see section 0.
- `[INSTR:name]` the instrument or script that produced it. `UNSOURCED-INSTR` means no instrument is recorded in the source.
- `[ADAPTER:AMD iGPU|NVIDIA|none]` the GPU the number came from. `none` means the figure is not adapter-dependent.
- `[RES:...]` the resolution, where recorded.
- `[W:sha]` the commit the figure is witnessed at, where recorded.
- **Provenance: mandatory, exactly one per figure row.** `[LANDED]` a run stands behind the number; `[DERIVED]` the number is derived by a named `path:line` chain in this repository, with no run behind it; `[STRUCK]` the number is withdrawn and the section names its replacement; `[REFUTED]` a run contradicted it; `[UNSOURCED]` no provenance could be established from the repository. A row carrying none of the five is not a figure: it is inadmissible and shall not be quoted. `[STRUCK]`, `[REFUTED]` and `[UNSOURCED]` are never quotable as measurements.
- **Lifecycle modifiers: optional, and they ride alongside provenance, never in its place.** `[PLANNED]`, `[UNBUILT]`, `[AWAITING-RE-DERIVATION]`, `[INVALIDATED]`.
- `[UNSOURCED-INSTR]` is the only spelling for "no instrument recorded", and it belongs in the `[INSTR:...]` slot, never as a free tag.
- **Figure row, decidable.** A figure row is a top-level bullet (a `- ` at column 0) in sections 0..12 that quotes a number and carries a figure slot (`[STAT:]`, `[INSTR:]`, `[ADAPTER:]`, `[RES:]` or `[W:]`); a bullet quoting a number with no slot and no provenance is prose, not a figure. An indented sub-bullet inherits its parent row's provenance and carries no tag of its own; the entries of section 13 are open questions, not figures. `scripts/lib/verify-doc-claims.mjs` fails the gate on any figure row carrying other than exactly one provenance tag.

No per-pass GPU timing is quotable as a GPU-bound measure. Section 0 holds every figure that rests on one.
