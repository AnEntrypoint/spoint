## Planet-wide multiplayer (`src/shared/clusterAssignment.js`, `src/sharding/`)

Dynamic **proximity clusters**, one server world per cluster, each its own flat chart, several worlds per worker — never one process per player. The Jolt wasm heap is **fixed at 134217728 B (128 MiB)**, never grows; exceeding it raises `Aborted(OOM)` in `new J.JoltInterface` (`src/physics/World.js:60`), killing every world in the process. `1157976 B` is resident before any world; a co-located client collision mirror spends `11278387 B`. A world's cost is ~98% **reservation, not content**: `10645288 + 33*mMaxBodies + 88*mMaxBodyPairs + 336*mMaxContactConstraints` (`src/physics/joltLimits.js`); content is static 266 B, dynamic 392 B, character 416 B, 65x65 heightfield 16 KB, grounded player 2144 B. So the density lever is **`joltLimits`, not world count**: `CLUSTER_SERVER_JOLT_LIMITS = 8192/8192/2048` (`src/sharding/ClusterServerWorld.js`), marginal **11.799 MiB** (12372064 B; was 19.301 MiB), `CLUSTER_HEAP_WORLD_CEILING` **5 -> 8**; 10 cluster worlds ran in one process at 60 Hz with 9.4 MiB left where 7 aborted. A full single world passes no `joltLimits` and keeps 10240/65536/10240. Peak at the largest allowed cluster: 3587 bodies, 329-516 manifolds.

The three limits are not the same kind of cap and two fail SILENTLY (measured 2026-10-06; undersizing does not abort): `maxContactConstraints` under requirement drops contacts with no signal (3072 statics + 512 dynamics rest at Y=-0.22 with 512, fall to Y=-49 with 256); `maxBodies` under population truncates silently; **`maxBodyPairs` is a queue batch, not a cap** — 5560 concurrent manifolds give bit-identical trajectories at 8 and at 65536 while still costing 88 B/slot. Only the wasm HEAP aborts. Size the first two from a measured peak with headroom; make overshoot loud.

Assignment is pure, deterministic (positions only, order independent, hysteretic, antipodal-safe), 2-4 Hz: O(n^2) is 0.16 ms at 16 players, 8.9 ms at 1024. Load is not why we partition — chart validity is (two players 100 km apart read 51.83 deg of slope). `resolveClusterConfig` refuses a link below the relevance ring or the longest weapon range, and a radius past `WALKABLE_CHART_LIMIT_DEG`. A chart-local point at or beyond the planet radius throws `ChartRangeError` rather than returning flat ground.
