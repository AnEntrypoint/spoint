project/underwater-tint-shared-sea-uniform: client/core/UnderwaterTint.js drives the sea surface through shared uniforms spointSea vec4 (seaY at the anchor in authoritative coords, planet radius R, enabled) and spointSeaShift vec3 (floating-origin shift, synced from window.__floatingOrigin in a chained scene.onBeforeRender) and exports SEA_SURFACE_GLSL/spointSeaSurfaceY (exact sphere, authoritative) plus adoptSeaUniforms; SSR.js embeds the same GLSL for its sea band. Registered on UniformsLib.fog and every ShaderLib entry; setSeaLevelY writes in place. Never re-splice chunk text after install (three's program cache ignores chunk text; see project/three-shaderchunk-edit-needsupdate-noop). Enabled=0 means disabled. Witness: pole under water, tint threshold = sphere-2 m within 0.5/1.9/0.5 cm at 300 m/6 km/9.5 km after rebases. Gotcha: WetnessTint's fog_vertex objectNormal line breaks MeshBasicMaterial programs, so witness poles must use a normal-bearing material (Lambert emissive).
