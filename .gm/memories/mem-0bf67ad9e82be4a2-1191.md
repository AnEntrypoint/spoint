Threshold-tuning is never a substitute for root-causing. aim_sillos.glb's degenerate-triangle defect resurfaced after an earlier fix (EPS_AREA=1e-6 in commit cd110b88, render-path only) because (1) the physics loader (src/physics/GLBLoader.js/ShapeBuilder.js) was never given the same fix, and (2) even the existing threshold was too tight for this asset's real defect scale -- 26 real slivers survived at 1e-6. Before touching the number again: live-tested spatial vertex welding (0.1mm-10mm cell sizes) and meshoptimizer's own simplifyPrune/simplify as real structural fixes -- neither worked, both operate on different invariants (topology/error, not raw area) than the actual defect. Only then derived a new threshold from a REAL measured gap in the mesh's own triangle-area histogram (largest real defect 7.26e-5 m^2, smallest legitimate triangle 1.12e-4 m^2, clean ~50% gap between) -- EPS_AREA=1e-4 sits inside that verified gap. Applied identically to src/physics/ShapeBuilder.js and packages/streaming-gltf/tools/bake-cluster.mjs.
