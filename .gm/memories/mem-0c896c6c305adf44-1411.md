A heightfield's interpolated height disagrees with the exact solver by an amount that is the flat-chart tilt term, not interpolation error and not a solver/sampler disagreement. Measured on tps-game (radius 63600 m, 256 m field, N=128, spacing 2.0157 m, exact CPU solver, 200-400 interior points per field, bilinear with the same formula preparedSurfaceY uses): max error 0.2452 m at the anchor, 0.2619 m at 1 km, 0.3708 m at 10 km, 4.0304 m at 60 km, against a spacing * tan(distance / radius) prediction of 0 / 0.0317 / 0.3196 / 2.7798 m over a roughly 0.21 m solver floor. Three-way separation: (a) the prediction tracks the growth once the angle is large; (b) halving the spacing multiplies the error by 0.47-0.65, not 0.25, so it is first order in spacing (a slope times spacing) rather than O(spacing^2) interpolation; (c) re-anchoring the chart on the same physical ground collapses 4.0304 m to 0.2084 m, which a solver disagreement or terrain roughness could not do. Bound to assume: about 0.35 m max and 0.16 m p95 inside a cell-keyed chart (measured at the worst cell angle 2.26 deg, d 2506 m), growing as 0.21 m + spacing * tan(distance / radius) once the anchor is further away - 0.37 m at 10 km, 4.0 m at 60 km. Every ground answer taken inside a field inherits it: spawn placement, kill plane, steering clamp. Shrinking the spacing is the weak lever (linear); re-anchoring is the strong one (19x).
