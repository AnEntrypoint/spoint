project/minimap-cell-height-was-chart-local-2026-10-02: src/shared/MinimapBiome.js sampleMinimapCell used to return frame.groundHeightLocal(x,z), which is the chart-LOCAL render Y = elevation - frame.anchorHeight - sphereDropBelowTangent(r^2, radius+elevation). That is chart-dependent: the same world point read in two charts differed by the anchors' height difference plus the drop term (measured -3521.6 m vs -2042.7 m vs 0.0 m for one point 20 deg from its anchor), so every reanchor shifted the whole minimap and its relief shading (shadeHeightGrid gradients picked up the drop bowl's r/R radial slope: 0.17 at 11 km, 0.53 at 28 deg), and even the fixed-anchor bake mis-read up to 951 m of fake depth at the edge of a 15.9 km minimap, misclassifying land as water. Fixed by returning elevationAtLocal(frame, x, h, z): exact elevation above sea level, chart-independent to <0.0004 m, identical in the flat-world radius=Infinity path. Biome colour and the land mask were already chart-independent because they compare against waterlineLocalY. MINIMAP_BAKE_CODE_VERSION hashes MinimapBiome.js, so bakes invalidate themselves.
