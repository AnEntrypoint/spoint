project/measurement-world-static-geometry-absent-in-inline-worlds: a harness or script that boots an inline world can load it WITHOUT its static box geometry, because box-static lives in src/stdlib-apps and the pre-commit appsDirs omitted that directory. Any measurement of wall contacts, wall-plane hints, blocking, prediction quality or displacement taken in such a world measures a world with no walls: it reports zero contacts and free movement, and reads as a legitimate 'symptom does not reproduce'. Witnessed 2026-10-02 on the spawn-w-forward row: one run reported raw=[] with zero contacts and 4.452 m of forward travel at the tps spawn, while a run with the geometry present at the same spawn reported two raw wall contacts both n=(0,-1), a byte-identical duplicate plane pair on 255/255 ticks and 0.000 displacement with velocity [0,0,7]. Before trusting any contact or movement measurement, assert the static-geometry apps are in appsDirs and that at least one contact is observed where one is expected; treat a zero-contact result as 'geometry probably missing' until proven otherwise.
