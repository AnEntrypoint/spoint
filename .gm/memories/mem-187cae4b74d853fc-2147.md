project/tsl-geomorph-round-tie-mismatch-2026-10-05: geomorph comparison. Morph factor, distance metric (patch vertex to eye in planet units), threshold = patch size * morphSplitDist * morphDistFactor, 2x end, smoothstep, skirt/level gates and the morph-before-normal-taps order are the same in terrain.glsl:294-312 and terrain-material-tsl.js:119-129. The mismatch is the parent-grid snap: legacy coarseAbs = round(absLocal / parentCell) * parentCell with parentCell = (2.0*size)/uGrid (terrain.glsl:307-309); TSL used floor(absLocal/parentCell + 0.5)*parentCell with parentCell = size*2*gridInv. Patch vertices sit at ox + i*size/9 and parentCell = 2*size/9 with ox a multiple of size, so absLocal/parentCell = (9*(k-R/size)+i)/2: half of all vertices are exact rounding ties. In a float32 emulation over level 11 and 12 patches 376 of 1024 sampled vertices land exactly on .5; round-half-even (ANGLE d3d11 HLSL round_ne, WGSL round) vs TSL half-up disagree for 166 of 1024 (16 percent) and half-away vs half-up for 190, so a sixth of the vertices of every morphing patch snapped to a different parent vertex (one parent cell, 13.8 m at level 11) in TSL than in legacy, changing nearest-ground heights and the finite-difference normals that reliefShade amplifies; the reciprocal multiply by gridInv additionally shifts the quotient by an ulp. AMD data: TSL left-lower relief shift +4.96 with geomorph on vs +1.16 off, gap to legacy -5.85 vs -1.37. Fix: round and division by gridSize in terrain-material-tsl.js (WGSL round is half-to-even; GLSL round on ANGLE d3d11 is round_ne). Pre-registered: with geomorph on, TSL-grey minus legacy-grey at default reliefShade within +-1.5 on terrain, grass and left-lower on both GPUs, and the geomorph on/off gap in the left-lower relief shift <= 0.3 on AMD (was 3.8). Caveat: legacy tie handling is implementation-defined in GLSL; if this prediction misses, the legacy behaviour is half-away or noise-determined and the TSL snap must be made explicit by vertex-index parity instead. The left-edge -8 on AMD is TSL-only (NVIDIA left edge +0.07, legacy AMD equals NVIDIA) and untouched by geomorph on or off.
