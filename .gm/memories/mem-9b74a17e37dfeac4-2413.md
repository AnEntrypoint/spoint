project/tsl-terrain-browner-mechanism-legacy-lighting-2026-09-30: Why TSL terrain reads browner than legacy (AMD, tps-game, pose (223.27,539.02) yaw 5.24 pitch -0.25, tod 0.4 clear): sand legacy RGB 182,164,109 vs TSL 150,129,94 (18% darker, less saturated). Not fog (scene Fog is linear 4.4-8.8 km) and not the splat textures (identical; mip chain verified by compute readback on NVIDIA). Mechanism: legacy terrain.glsl does its OWN lighting and grade: sunIrr = ATM_SOLAR_IRRADIANCE*transmittanceToSun(r=6360.5,mu)*n.l * host shadow, lit = albedo*(sunIrr*1.25 + skyIrrBalanced)/pi + albedo*0.14 + (0.02,0.026,0.038) with skyIrr = solar*0.075*smoothstep(-0.1,0.25,muS)*mix(1,rayleigh/rayleigh.x,0.4)*0.5(1+n.up) balanced 35% to grey * skyFill*(0.85,0.92,1.1); normal exaggerated nLit = normalize(up + (mix(vNrm,up,0.05)-up)*reliefShade 6) then + texDn; aerial perspective atm_marchRadiance with apGate smoothstep(3,120 atm-km) (atm km = world m * 6360/R, so 30 m..1.2 km at R 63600) and hazeMul 0.4; dayShade/nightFill; exposure 1.05, ACES, lookSat 1.25, lookContrast, pow 1/2.2. TSL terrain instead uses three MeshStandard PBR lit by the scene sun (1.54) + ambient under renderer ACES with no saturation grade and no relief exaggeration. A port (terrain-lighting-tsl.js: custom LightingModel on a MeshLambertNodeMaterial subclass, sun term gated on the three sun direction and shadow = luminance(lightColor)/sun luminance, grade in material.outputNode = f(output)) was attempted and REVERTED unfinished: the direct() sun term contributed nothing (A/B: sunLum 1e9 left pixels identical) and the terrain rendered flat; not yet root-caused (suspects: normalView/lightDirection inside the custom model, shadowK ~0). WIP copy kept in the session scratchpad legacy-lighting-wip/.
