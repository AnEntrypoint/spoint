project/planet-chart-anchor-lattice-density-vs-walkable-2026-10-02: Seamless circumnavigation reanchors the single flat chart to the nearest anchor of a lattice built by createChartAnchorLattice(radius, anchorsPerFace) (3 per cube face = 54 anchors, worst anchor-to-cell-corner angle 21.235 deg; 6 per face = 216 anchors, 11.309 deg). Reanchor fires when the player passes CHART_REANCHOR_ANGLE_DEG from the anchor (28 deg at 3/face, 14 at 6/face; threshold must exceed the worst cell-corner angle or the anchor thrashes). The chart contributes a curvature slope of about the current angle-from-anchor on top of real terrain relief, measured as chartExcessDeg = flat-chart ground slope minus the chart-independent terrain gradient: 27.0-29.6 deg at 3/face/28 deg, 12.1-13.7 deg at 6/face/14 deg, on tps-game R=63600. Doubling the lattice density roughly doubles reanchor events per circumnavigation (12 -> 24 on the equator route) and lifts walkable cube crossings from 17/60 to 22/60. Pick the density from the worst terrain relief you must still walk: relief itself reaches 74 deg on this world, so no lattice makes every point walkable.
