project/wall-plane-duplicate-witness-needs-seam-pose: the duplicate-wall-plane fix in src/physics/CharacterManager.js (f2ba59611f) is only exercised by a pose where ONE flat wall yields two contact points, and the shipped corner world's default spawn is not such a pose -- there the player touches nothing, so old and new agree on 325/325 ticks and the guard cannot fail. The discriminating pose is --at=-1.4,1.2,-4.4 with --hold=right,backward, which puts the capsule straddling the seam of two coplanar boxes: pre-fix packs [1,0] [1,0] (the perpendicular wall's slot starved) and post-fix packs [1,0] [0,1], differing on ~384 of 400 ticks. A second discriminating pose is the tps spawn (-15, 3.64, -10.14), where pre-fix packs two byte-identical planes on 255/255 ticks. Any regression guard for this fix must use one of those two, never the corner world's default spawn. Also note the dedup has nil client-visible effect by itself: duplicate planes are always exactly identical because d = n*position comes from the same position, so _rememberWalls already collapsed them.
