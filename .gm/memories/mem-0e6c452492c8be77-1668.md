CALIBRATION FACT: an arm that measures about 0.74 corrections per ack is push-OFF regardless of the flag passed -- an earlier row recorded 0.737 'push-on' against 0.717 'control', i.e. push-off against push-off, and wrongly concluded the push was harmful. It was reverted, not committed.
