A chart reanchor is a change of basis, not a physical rotation: createChartTransfer's m[r][c]=to[r].from[c] maps from-basis components to to-basis components and point() lifts by fromLift/rotates/subtracts toLift, so WORLD position, velocity and forward direction are invariant. Carrying only the yaw breaks that invariant: a forward vector horizontal in the old chart (perpendicular to up_from) acquires an up_to component of up to sin(angle(up_from,up_to)) after the reanchor, so reconstructing it as horizontal in the new chart rotates the true world forward by that angle (measured -39 deg for a 30 deg reanchor). createChartTransfer therefore exposes look(yaw,pitch) -> {yaw,pitch} and NOT a yaw-only helper; carry the pair. The induced pitch is real and not a bug: in the flat-chart design gravity is -up in chart-local coordinates, so reanchoring rotates the effective gravity direction by the reanchor angle and the character's up genuinely changes; what must stay continuous is the world-space forward, which look() preserves exactly. Witness: harness reports worldForwardJumpDeg (look carry, must be ~0) next to yawOnlyForwardJumpDeg (the yaw-only counterfactual) and inducedPitchDeg.
