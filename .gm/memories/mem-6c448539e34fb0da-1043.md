project/wall-plane-hint-orientation-and-dedup: character wall-plane hints are packed as d = n*position, so every emitted plane passes through the player and free space is n*p - d >= 0 (slideAlongWalls in src/shared/characterStep.js). A contact carrying the negated normal of the wall it came from therefore forbids the direction the server is actually moving and pins the player at zero displacement with full velocity -- the spawn-w-forward symptom. src/physics/CharacterManager.js _captureWalls now keeps a contact only when the character is on that plane's free side (dot of the normal with character-minus-contact-point > 0) and merges normals within WALL_MERGE_DOT_MIN = 0.98, so one wall is emitted once. Dropping rather than flipping is deliberate: a deeply penetrating character would flip the wrong way and block its own escape. Verified with a box wall: 59 m slid along it, 0.25% mispredict, 0.0 cm max pop, identical to pre-fix, and predictCharacterStep keeps the tangential component (0.05 m/tick) while zeroing the into-plane one.
