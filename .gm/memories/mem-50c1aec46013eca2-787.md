A freshly spawned player is not targetable for `spawnInvulnMs` (arena-combat and tps-game both declare 1500), applied on join and on respawn via `isTargetable` returning false while `invuln` is in the future. So a harness that fires immediately after join produces a health sequence like [100, 100, 60, 40, 20, 0] -- the FIRST SHOT IS CORRECTLY IGNORED, not eaten by hit resolution. Witnessed 2026-10-02 in arena-combat with two real clients: firing immediately gave [100,100,60,40,20,0,0]; adding a 6-second settle before the first shot gave [80,60,40,20,0,0] with hit=5, kill=1 and respawn restoring 100. Before diagnosing a 'missed shot' as a hitscan or lag-compensation defect, check whether the shot landed inside spawnInvulnMs. This is a real trap for any automated combat witness.
