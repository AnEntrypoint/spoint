## Resolved mutable: webgpu-terrainbackdrop-renderplanet-fwdvector-broken-for-manual-camera-pose

ROOT CAUSE FOUND AND FIXED (real fix, not a threshold tweak). client/core/TerrainBackdrop.js renderPlanet() line ~181 had `const _fwdYSigned = isWebGPU ? -_fwd.y : _fwd.y` feeding the east/up/north fE projection -- this WebGPU-only Y-flip is mathematically wrong whenever frame.up != [0,1,0] (i.e. any anchorDir other than the trivial pole; tps-game's live frame.up read as [-0.641,0.256,0.724], not [0,1,0]). Live proof via gm browser verb, real Chromium/D3D12/Dawn WebGPU device at http://localhost:8099/?singleplayer&world=tps-game&webgpu=1 (SW unregistered + caches cleared first): (1) numeric proof -- computed TerrainBackdrop's own fE-based target vs the algebraically-exact ground truth (frame.localToWorld applied directly to eye_local and target_local=eye_local+fwd_local*dist); with the flip, diffMag=1227.92 at a 1000m lookahead (huge); with the flip removed, diffMag=7.28e-12 (exact zero) -- for the SAME real camera.getWorldDirection() output at a steep manual pose. (2) closed-loop proof using the REAL shared M4.lookAt (packages/mapspinner/src/gl-render-mat4.js, used by both GL and WebGPU) plus real frame.east/up/north: projected the true forward point through each candidate view basis and measured off-axis angle. Flipped: 11.6 deg off-center at a small TPS-like pitch (fwd=[0.91,-0.10,0.40], survives inside a ~70deg FOV so gameplay looked unaffected) and 75.76 deg off-center at a steep aerial pitch (fwd=[0.558,-0.614,0.558], falls completely outside the frustum -> pure sky, exactly matching the reported symptom). Unflipped: ~4e-12 deg (exact center) at BOTH pitches. Also confirmed M4.lookAt (shared, backend-agnostic) and perspectiveZeroToOne (already proven elsewhere to exactly match THREE's own WebGPUCoordinateSystem projection) have no genuine WebGPU-specific Y-handedness need, so the flip added in commit fcdddfa2 was a wrong band-aid for a since-superseded symptom.
