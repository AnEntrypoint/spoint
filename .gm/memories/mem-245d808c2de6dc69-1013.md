project/spoint-core-rust-js-twins-byte-identical: packages/spoint-core/src/lib.rs holds Rust twins of JS math that must stay byte-identical, the first slice of the ugc-wasm-core-extraction plan. pack_quat/unpack_quat pair with SnapshotEncoder.js; fnv1a_32 with msgpack.js _computeStructHash; fnv1a_fold_f64 with LockstepChecksum.js foldFloat64, which folds raw little-endian f64 bits and maps -0 to +0 so both runtimes agree; mul_quat/rot_vec with math.js. Change each pair together or wire hashes and lockstep checksums diverge between runtimes. Related shapes moved out of source: the GPU-instance-batching example declares mvPosition at outer scope the way the project_vertex chunk does because fog and later chunks read it; FXC cold-compiles the mapspinner bake program in 30 to 90 seconds, so bake uses an explicit fxcColdCompileBudgetMs poll instead of one bounded retry; lab.mjs parks the sun at sunLatBase 0.35 because a near-overhead sun reads flat and gentle relief only self-shades at an oblique angle.
