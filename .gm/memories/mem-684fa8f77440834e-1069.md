project/mapspinner-planet-scale-invariant-harness: planet.html camera units are about 998 m at every _planetScale. RADIUS=6371*s units, the planet radius is 6360000*s m and is passed to initMapspinnerPlanet (without it any scale other than 0.001 desyncs camera and planet). maxR=MAX_CAM_R=6000 R (38226 units at 0.001), so the rungs were never clamped; the real defect was rungs 1000x too high. GPU ground heights scale exactly with radius (-35..27.8 m at 0.001, -35000..27785 m at 1, landFrac 0.398 at both). Witness thresholds and parks are therefore written at the 6360 km reference and scaled by refM/_planetScale, and RUNGS are earth-reference km times _planetScale (commit 40cc983d). Use render.sampleGroundMSync for witness loops: the async sampleGroundM returns the previous call's value. sampleGroundMSync currently poisons the next async collision read, lifting the camera to the 15 km cap (row glrender-sync-probe-poisons-async-collision). gm cdp's default page is shared across agents, so drive an isolated Chrome (own --user-data-dir) for planet witnesses.
