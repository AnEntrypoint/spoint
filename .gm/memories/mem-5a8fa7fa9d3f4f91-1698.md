Planet chart re-selection can be keyed on the player's cell (chartNeedsReanchorForCell + reanchorChartFor({cellTrigger:true}) in src/shared/chartAnchor.js) instead of the continuous 28 deg angle test. Cell-keyed selection needs no wire field and no hysteresis margin: the anchor becomes a pure function of a discrete cell, so client predicted and server authoritative positions agree. The margin to quote is cell width vs prediction error (16650 m at anchorsPerFace 6, 33300 m at 3, against sub-metre error), NOT the angular margin (16.7 deg at A6, 6.8 deg at A3). Verified non-thrashing: after reanchor, dist2 to the cell anchor is exactly 0. MEASURED 2026-10-02, 60 crossings / 16 routes, walkable-out-of-60: A3 threshold 17, A6 threshold 22, A3 cell 9, A6 cell 24. Cell triggering is WORSE at anchorsPerFace 3 - you re-anchor the instant you cross a 30 deg cell boundary, ~15 deg from the new centre, whereas the 28 deg threshold re-anchors later when you are already deep in the new cell. It only wins once cells are halved to 15 deg (boundary angle ~7.5 deg). A6+cell also collapses the minimap seam error (maxRouteVsTruthBefore 36 -> 1, meanRouteSeamDiff 0.0024 -> 0.0001, maxSeamHeightDiffM 27.69 -> 0.01 m) and halves induced camera pitch at a re-anchor (41.4 deg -> 21.1 deg). Cost: re-anchors 202 -> 590 over 6400 km of routes = one per ~11 km of walking, roughly one per 26 min at 7 m/s - quote it per km travelled, because the harness's 10 km sample step is the same order as the cell and makes it look like one per sample. Unchanged at every density and trigger: 24-27 genuine terrain failures above 45 deg, so circumnavigation walkability is not closed by a lattice or trigger choice.
