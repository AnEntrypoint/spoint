project/geomorph-parent-snap-use-integer-indices-not-float-round: a vertex grid whose spacing is a patch-size fraction puts half of all vertices exactly on a rounding tie when snapping to the parent grid (absLocal/parentCell = g/2 + integer, g the global vertex index), so the float round() result depends on tie rule and on one-ulp rounding of absLocal/parentCell, which differs between backends and GPUs (legacy through ANGLE d3d11 was identical on AMD and NVIDIA; TSL WGSL round matched legacy on NVIDIA but not on AMD: AMD left-lower relief shift with geomorph on stayed +4.0 vs +1.16 off). Fix in tsl/geomorph-snap-tsl.js parentGridPosition: vertex index i = floor(grid*9+0.5), patch index k = floor((ox+R)/size+0.5), g = 9k+i, parent index = 2*roundHalfEven(g/2) done with floor and parity on integer-valued floats, parent position = parent*size/9 - R; no float round and no reciprocal. Verified: the emitted WGSL contains floor only (built in Node through WGSLNodeBuilder), and an f32 emulation of the emitted arithmetic matches the exact integer rule on 100000 random vertices over levels 2 to 15 with 0 mismatches. Against the legacy f32 expression with round-to-nearest-even the integer rule agrees on 100 percent of non-tie vertices and 89 percent of tie vertices (the other 11 percent of ties are legacy results decided by an ulp of absLocal/parentCell, which no implementation-independent rule reproduces); the previous TSL floor(x+0.5) differed from the integer rule on 25 percent of all vertices. Pre-registered: NVIDIA unchanged (terrain +-1.5, left-lower +-1); AMD grey terrain within +-1.5 of legacy and the AMD left-lower geomorph on-vs-off relief-shift gap <= 0.3 (was 2.84); if AMD still misses, geomorph is not the AMD term and the left-edge -7 and the -2 grey residual are separate AMD-only TSL terms.
