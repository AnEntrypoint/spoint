Symptom seen 2026-10-07: a lane editing src/netcode/SnapshotEncoder.js left SNAPSHOT_ENTITY_ENC_CODE_VERSION pinned at dd7da6939902 while its source hashed to 0a83555beaf3, so check exited 1 on a transform no other lane touched.
