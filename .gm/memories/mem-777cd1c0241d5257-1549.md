TickHandler INPUT_BACKLOG_DRAIN merges <=2 queued inputs into the last one (drops movement) and repeats lastInput on starved ticks. PredictionEngine.predict has no collisions, a floor at y=0 and gravity on every step. reconcile compares server@ackSeq against the CURRENT predicted state instead of predicted@ackSeq, so every moving snapshot corrects, and errorOffset is computed before resimulate.
