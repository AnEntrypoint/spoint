project/fire-placement-fuel-cannot-be-derived-in-the-integer-kernel: a fire cell's fuel cannot be recomputed from vegetation placement inside src/shared/fire/fireKernel.js. classify() (src/terrain/VegPlacement.js:170-234) is a float chain over the anchor field - groveAt noise, climateAt temp/humidity/erosion/seaBias, radialSlopeAt, paintedWeightsFor(frame.hashVersion) grass weight, treeline and reliefMarginScale, and rand(cellHash, k) for species/size - so its answer depends on the chart, the anchor field and float rounding, while the fire kernel is integer-only and its (face,I,J) cells are deliberately chart independent; src/shared/fire/fireLattice.js carries geometry only. The kernel can therefore only store an integer the host hands it, through a fuelAt(face, I, J) hook at tile creation, and a mirror with no anchorField cannot recompute it, so per-cell initial fuel has to travel in the keyframe (a TILE_ARRAYS entry past the interior bitmaps of version 4).
