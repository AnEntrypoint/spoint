LockstepGameLoop.tailOf(peer) must share the dropped peer inputs from simTick - inputDelayTicks, not from simTick. Two survivors are never more than inputDelayTicks apart (to simulate t a peer needs every peer input for t, emitted while that peer simulates t - inputDelayTicks), so that margin is exactly what a slower survivor needs to fill its gap up to the cut. Sharing only from simTick froze a peer that had never heard the dropped peer: with --deadLink=peer-c>peer-a it simulated 0 ticks in 20 s while the peer that could hear c ran on alone. The cut itself cannot be lowered to fix this: cut = max(reports) and must be at or above every survivor simTick, or a survivor would have to un-simulate ticks it already ran. inputDelayTicks > INPUT_RETAIN_TICKS (240) throws at construction so the margin always stays inside the retain window.
