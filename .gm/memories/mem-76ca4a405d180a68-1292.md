Planet wrap decision (2026-10-02): seamless circumnavigation is built by REANCHORING the single flat chart per cube-sphere face, never by radial gravity. The world stays ONE flat chart at anchorDir (tps-game R=63600 m), gravity stays the global [0,-18,0], the character's up stays +Y, and the 45 deg slope limit stays a flat-chart rule -- measurements show the flat chart is why ground tilts 18.3 deg at 20 km, 38.9 at 40 km and 45.4 at 45 km with no surface past 63.5 km, i.e. the chart itself is the limit, not gravity. Radial gravity was rejected because Jolt has one global gravity vector, so per-body radial gravity means applying a force to every dynamic body every step, and it would force a rewrite of the character controller's up-axis assumption, physics tuning, terrain placement, colliders and the minimap, all of which are chart-based. Reanchor instead crosses a face boundary by applying one rigid rotation to ALL state (position, velocity, camera yaw, floating origin, in-flight snapshot/wire state, loaded chunk sets) so the world looks identical across the seam and only coordinates change; the cost to engineer is the reanchor hitch (chunks re-bake for the new anchorDir), mitigated by streaming the new chart's chunks before the switch and doing the transform in one frame.
