project/gm-browser-gl-geterror-hook-kills-fps: the gm browser verb injects own-property wrappers for gl.drawArrays/drawElements/drawArraysInstanced/drawElementsInstanced that call gl.getError() after every draw (window.__gmGlDrawCalls). On tps-game (RTX 3060, 159 draws) renderer.render took 41 ms/frame = 22 fps; after `const gl=__renderer.getContext(); for (const k of ['drawArrays','drawElements','drawArraysInstanced','drawElementsInstanced']) delete gl[k]` it is 10 ms = 81 fps. Delete those own props at the start of every fps/perf measurement dispatch until gm makes the hook opt-in.
