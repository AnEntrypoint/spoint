## Resolved mutable: grass-near-lod-postcondition

Confirmed live via client/core/Grass.js grass.update() direct calls (real shipped module, no mock): BEFORE fix, commitChunk's useMid check compared player distance to the chunk's fixed CENTER (kcx*CH+CH*0.5), so a chunk's near tier was only reachable within an 8m disc around its geometric center inside a 32m chunk -- e.g. a player at a chunk corner (~21.2m from center) computed distSq=450>64, forcing useMid=true (mid tier) for the ENTIRE chunk regardless of how close the player actually stood to blades at that corner. FIXED (client/core/Grass.js commitChunk): distance is now computed to the chunk AABB's NEAREST POINT to the player (px/pz clamped into [kcx*CH,(kcx+1)*CH]x[kcz*CH,(kcz+1)*CH]), so standing anywhere inside a chunk gives distance 0 (always near tier) and standing near an edge extends near tier into the adjacent chunk by the LOD_NEAR_DIST margin, matching the intended near/mid split. Live A/B on the running server: after rebuildPlacement()+update() at chunk corner (-31,0,-31) (21.2m from that chunk's center, the exact case the old formula misclassified), grass._im.instancesCount=623 (near tier populated, imMid=0) -- proving the near tier is no longer structurally unreachable. A second call at (-16,0,-16) (nominal center) also gives _im.instancesCount=175>0. Both confirm the fix; the corner case specifically proves it (old center-distance=21.2m>8 would have forced mid-tier there).
