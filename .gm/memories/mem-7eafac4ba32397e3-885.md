gm git_diff compares raw bytes and ignores core.autocrlf, so a file whose DISK line endings differ from its BLOB shows as a whole-file rewrite (bundle-client.mjs reported 176/117 for a 12/71 change). Blobs in spoint are per-file inconsistent: scripts/bundle-client.mjs, scripts/bundle-worker.mjs and package.json are LF; src/sdk/ServerBoot.js and src/sdk/StaticHandler.js are CRLF. Before committing an edited file, convert it to its blob ending and re-run git_diff --stat: a drop from ~N0 insertions/deletions to the real number proves the match.
