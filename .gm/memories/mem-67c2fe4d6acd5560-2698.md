Before fix: console captured 'GPUValidationError: Binding size (262144) of [Buffer bindingBufferundefined_UniformBuffer_20_(vertex)] is larger than the maximum uniform buffer binding size (65536)' and identical for size 131072 buffer_26 (bufErrCount=2, totalErrCount=5). Edits: client/core/GrassTSL.js (buffer->storage import+call, line 5 and 10-13), client/core/PlayerVATTSL.js (line 2 and 5-8), client/core/PlayerLODTSL.js (line 2 and 4-7).
