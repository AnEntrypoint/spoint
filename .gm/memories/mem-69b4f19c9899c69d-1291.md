project/prediction-wall-plane-hints-position-only: client prediction has no world geometry, so sliding along a static wall mispredicted 57% of acks (8.3 cm p95). Fix (wire v4): CharacterManager._captureWalls reads CharacterVirtual.GetActiveContacts() after ExtendedUpdate and keeps static, non-sensor, mHadCollision contacts whose normal |y| < 0.3 and whose contact point is above feet + maxStepHeight (so stair risers are excluded). Their xz normals ride the per-recipient me block as me[3] (src/shared/wallPlaneWire.js). The plane offset is computed from the quantized normal on the server, because recomputing it on the client from a quantized normal is wrong by (n - nq) dot p, which is huge at planet coordinates. PredictionEngine caches up to 6 planes with the tangent range where contact was seen, extends it by 1 m plus speed times the unacked horizon, drops a plane once the server is behind it (the wall ended) or 6 m away, and characterStep clamps POSITION only. Projecting velocity as well made things worse: the server's CharacterVirtual velocity is not projected, so clamping velocity diverged the acceleration. Measure with scripts/netcode-conditioner-harness.mjs --world=wall|wall-end|stairs --wallHints=on|off. Stairs (step-up) are not hinted: 3% mispredict, p95 about 1 cm.
