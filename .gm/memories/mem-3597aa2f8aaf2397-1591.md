Jolt heightfields quantize heights over the field's own [min,max]. Measured: raycast-vs-sample error is about range/30000 (0.001955 m at 64 m range, 0.000012 m at 0.125 m), and changing the min or max cell by as little as 1e-5 m re-anchors the quantization and moves the collider surface by up to range/5000 (13.1 mm at 64 m) anywhere in the field - 1e-6 m moves it 0, and 1e-3 m on a non-extreme cell moves it only locally (0.5 mm). Consequence: sub-mm sample differences (2-15 um was the reuse-build vs fresh-build gap, because buildField with reuse=true seeds from resident samples and solves only the rest) produce mm-level collider drift whenever they land on an extreme cell; two builds that should be identical raycast up to 14 mm apart. Jolt itself is bit-deterministic: bit-identical Float32Array plus same N/scale/position gives 0.000000 m over five arms. Fix 2026-10-06 (18d8f438): sampleTerrainGridChunked snaps every sample to 1 mm (HEIGHT_SNAP_PER_METRE = 1000); the + 0 is load-bearing because Math.round of a sample in (-0.5 mm, 0) yields -0, a different bit pattern than +0 in a Float32Array. Both field paths, buildField and prepareFields, go through it. Cost: the snapped collider sits up to 13.1 mm from the unsnapped one, but deterministically. Bound for any terrain-correctness claim: pin the resident field set and the field's extremes; mm-level collider comparisons are only meaningful between builds with identical extremes. The rendered surface comes from the GPU sampler, not from these samples, so physics is snapped and visuals are not - residual at most 0.5 mm.
