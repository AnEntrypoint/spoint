project/app-physics-addforce-is-impulse: ctx.physics.addForce(f) (src/apps/AppPhysics.js buildPhysicsAPI) calls World.addImpulse, i.e. an instantaneous impulse, not a per-second force -- callers scale by dt themselves. There is no ctx.physics.addImpulse/applyImpulse; off-centre impulses go through ctx.world.applyImpulse(entityId, impulse, worldPoint).
