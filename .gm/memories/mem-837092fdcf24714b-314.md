project/procgen-wfc-socket-keys-uppercase: src/procgen/WFC.js rulesFromSockets reads tile sockets by DIRS keys N/S/E/W (uppercase). Lowercase socket keys (e.g. in ProcgenPanel DEFAULT_WFC_TILES) read undefined===undefined for every pair and silently produce a fully-connected nonsense ruleset with no error thrown.
