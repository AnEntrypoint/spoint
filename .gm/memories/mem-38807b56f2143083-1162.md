project/snapshot-timeline-remote-interpolation: remote players render from src/client/SnapshotTimeline.js (replaced JitterBuffer/Kalman/SmoothInterpolation). Clock = min over a 90-sample window of (arrival - tick*tickMs); jitter = p80 of the offset above that min (p95 let TCP retransmit stalls at 5% loss inflate the delay to about 300 ms; stalls are bridged by <=100 ms velocity extrapolation, then hold); target delay = 1.5*snapshotInterval + jitter; render time advances in real time and converges on the target (4/s, resync above 200 ms). Players only: snapshot entity lists are deltas (only changed entities), so they can't be interpolated from the list. It holds at most 32 snapshots, under SnapshotProcessor SNAPSHOT_RELEASE_WINDOW 96 (pooled slots). app.js drives remote nodes per frame via SceneGraph.setRemotePlayerTransforms (direct, no EMA) ONLY when there is no TransformRing: singleplayer/host BrowserServer coalesces snapshots latest-wins, so the timeline saturates there; the ring gives authoritative per-frame transforms. The effective delay depends on the snapshot rate (20 Hz once RTT>50 under the current policy) and on server tick bunching.
