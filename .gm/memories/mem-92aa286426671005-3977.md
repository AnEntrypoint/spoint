THE FORK NUMBER. There is exactly ONE chart per world: setupTerrainStreaming calls createPlanetFrame once with a single tcfg.anchorDir and getCenters() collects ALL players into one ring set (call sites src/sdk/ServerAPI.js:78, src/sdk/WorkerEntry.js:109, src/apps/AppContext.js:446, src/sdk/EditorHandlers.js:462 - all authoritative-side, none in client/). For two players at angular separation Delta the best shared anchor is the midpoint so each sits at theta = Delta/2, and the flat-chart term adds theta to the terrain slope: walkable iff terrainTilt + Delta/2 <= 45 deg. Measured over the 60 face-edge/corner crossings (identical across all four configs - it is the same terrain): terrain tilt min 28.87, p25 39.08, median 44.22, p75 51.43, max 74.23 deg -> Delta_max = 2*(45-terrainTilt) has median 1.56 deg, p25 11.84 deg, best 32.26 deg, and 28 of 60 crossings are 0 (terrain alone exceeds 45 deg, no chart helps). Party spread that survives: 15 deg -> 8 of 60 crossings, 30 deg -> 1 of 60, 45 deg and above -> 0. So a shared flat chart is a singleplayer mechanism: median tolerable party spread is 1.56 deg (~1.7 km on a 63.6 km-radius planet), not the quarter-of-the-planet the 45 deg tilt heuristic suggests. CAVEAT: measured at the 60 crossings, the hard cases; a whole-surface sweep would be gentler and is what the two-walker extension would measure. Re-anchoring does NOT fix this by improving the trigger - A6 already holds theta to 9.57-9.80 deg (cell worst 11.31 deg), so tilt is not the binding term; the single shared frame is.
