project/mapspinner-rgba32f-not-rgb32f-renderable: on ANGLE/D3D11, RGB32F is not color-renderable (FRAMEBUFFER_INCOMPLETE_ATTACHMENT) even with EXT_color_buffer_float; RGBA32F is. gl-render.js uploads the atmosphere transmittance/scattering LUTs as RGBA32F (alpha wasted) so FBO readback/blit diagnostics work. R32F/RGBA32F LINEAR filtering needs OES_texture_float_linear, else NEAREST.
