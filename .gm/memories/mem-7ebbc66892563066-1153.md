project/tsl-display-referred-inverse-aces-red-clamp-2026-10-05: after the 8-step sky march the TSL sky crop was (83.1,176.3,235.8) vs legacy (56.2,176.3,235.8) on both GPUs. Root cause is display-referred-tsl.js invertAcesFilmic ending in max(acesInputInverse*y, 0): a saturated blue display colour such as (56,176,236) inverts to scene-linear (-0.034, 0.346, 1.395), red negative because it lies outside the AP1/ACES gamut, and clamping red to 0 makes the renderer's own ACES re-encode it to (82.7,175.6,235.7). A Node round trip of three's ACES forward plus the analytic inverse reproduced the measured TSL numbers to 0.4 levels, and without the clamp returns 55.9,175.6,235.7. Other probes: 115,210,245 -> 140.9 clamped vs 114.6 unclamped; 161,227,250 -> 180.0 vs 160.5. Fix: drop the max so negative red survives in the half-float scene colour (shared by grass, terrain and water outputs; any saturated blue was affected). Pre-registered prediction for the next sky parity boot: TSL sky-crop mean (56.2+-1.5, 176.3+-1, 235.8+-1), meanAbs <= 2. Watch for: a post pass that clamps the scene colour to >=0 before tone mapping would resurrect the error.
