project/loaded-box-timer-noise-floor-exceeds-37-percent: on this shared box a millisecond-per-tick counter is not a measurement. scripts/cpu-skip-witness.mjs run twice back to back on the identical build moved _lastCollisionMs 0.310 -> 0.423 ms/tick (+37%) and _lastInteractMs 0.0246 -> 0.0310 (+26%), while integer work-unit counters in the same two runs moved under 1%. Any ms/tick delta below roughly 40% on this box is noise, so decide rows on counted work units (re-buckets, distance tests, _encodeEntity calls, allocated buffers, memo hits) and report the ms figure as unmeasured rather than as a win or a regression. Load also moves the thing being measured: the byte-identical fraction of packed entity bin records was 0.378 at ~78 ticks/s, 0.254 at 64 and 0.109 at 52, because a starved tick loop gives every prop a larger dt and therefore more movement per tick.
