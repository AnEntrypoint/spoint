project/tps-game-spawn-ground-snap-live-band: groundSnapSpawnPoint (src/sdk/ServerHandlers.js, initial join via ctx.worldSpawnPoint) and getAvailableSpawnPoint (apps/tps-game/server.js, respawn against collectSpawnPoints' 4 placed spawn-point entities) both ground-raycast-validate spawn Y, but pre-fix the raycast band was anchored to the STALE stored Y with a narrow window (groundSnapSpawnPoint +/-20, getAvailableSpawnPoint only -5..+10) -- any terrain-height delta bigger than that band (seed change via continentalBias, or any future terrain edit) fell through to a fully unvalidated raw-candidate fallback, stranding the player buried or floating with no correction (matches the y<-20 fall-safety loop). Fixed: both now compute heightHint=max(storedY, ctx.terrainHeightAt(x,z)) and raycast from heightHint+20 downward 2000 units, falling back to ctx.terrainHeightAt(x,z) (never the raw stale candidate) on a raycast miss; apps/tps-game/server-app.js also ground-snaps ctx.state.spawnPoints once at setup() via the new exported groundSnapCandidate helper (server.js) so powerup placement (which reads spawnPoints[i] directly) inherits the corrected Y too. Live-witnessed seed=99 (spawns stable, no fall) and seed=1337 (unaffected regression check). Separate, still-open, NOT fixed by this: project/tps-game-sillos-y-offset -- apps/world/tps-game.js's env-sillos position (Y=10.31) is a hardcoded constant tuned for the old seed-1337 terrain height at origin; at seed 99 the arena structure itself is now substantially buried under the risen terrain even though its spawn points correctly ground-snap onto whatever surface is actually there post-fix.
