project/interact-cooldown-tick-indexed: src/apps/AppRuntimeTick.js _interactCooldowns stores expiry as runtime.currentTick + ticks (ms converted with that tick's this.deltaTime), never Date.now(). RollbackLoop.resimulateFrom replays ticks later in wall time, so a wall-clock expiry flips the cooldown decision on resimulate and desyncs rollback. Any new per-tick gameplay timer in AppRuntime needs the same tick keying.
