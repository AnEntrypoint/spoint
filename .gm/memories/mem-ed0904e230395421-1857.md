client/core/Weather.js _groundHeight memo: what it bought and what it did not. Before the fix every respawned rain droplet and snowflake called frame.groundHeightLocal at a brand-new random position (MAX_PARTICLES 3000 rain plus 3000 snow inside a 22 m box), measured at 1.17M calls in 45 s of walking and 44.24% of the main thread in msH3i/msSnoise3/composeHeight. Live attribution by stack sampling (scripts/perf-run.mjs --probe-heights wraps frame.groundHeightLocal and samples 1 in 500 call stacks): 65% Weather _respawnDroplet/_respawnFlake, ~25% src/terrain/PlacementChart.js surfaceAlongDir via VegPlacement.classify and RockPlacement.classify, ~10% scattered. sampler.heightAt and frame._patchPrefetch were 0 calls, so ColliderStreamer's per-chunk sampling is not the hot path. After memoizing on a 1 m grid (cap 32768 entries, canary re-sample every 500 ms clearing the cache when the height source moves): clean 120 s A/B at matched travel (660 m vs 674 m), train=0 ours=0, WebGPU RTX 3060, refreshHz 143.9: fps 36.0 -> 71.6, p50 27.78 -> 13.97 ms, p95 48.65 -> 34.68, p99 69.46 -> 41.71, height-sampling share 44.24% -> 14.39%, groundHeightLocal calls 3.10M -> 0.90M. The residual 0.90M is the untouched ~25% placement path: the memo lives in Weather and only Weather callers go through it, so PlacementChart/VegPlacement/RockPlacement in src/terrain still pay full price. Raising the cell to 2 m and the cap to 131072 is a NO-OP (measured: htPct 14.76 vs 14.39, calls 857k vs 902k, p50 13.98 vs 13.97) -- the limiter was never cap eviction, so do not re-try that. Next real win is memoizing or batching the placement-path samples in src/terrain, which is another agent's surface. GL calls per frame reads 0 on every WebGPU arm because the rig instruments WebGL2RenderingContext and WebGPU exposes no WebGL context; that number needs ?legacygl=1.
