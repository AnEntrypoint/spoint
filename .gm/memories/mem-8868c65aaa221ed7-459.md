gm spool dispatch files must be named in/<verb>/<session_id>-<random8>.txt (for example f73df536-...-muw6fkl5.txt, as in in/cdp and in/memorize-fire). Files named in/<verb>/<N>.txt with a plain number are swept out of the directory without ever executing: no dispatch.end event, no out file, and the PRD or commit silently does not happen. The out file is out/<verb>-<session_id>-<random8>.json. If a dispatch appears to vanish, check the filename form first.
