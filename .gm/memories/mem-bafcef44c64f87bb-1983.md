project/fire-kernel-trusted-shrunk-indices-and-declared-lengths-2026-10-06: three fire defects found in one adversarial sweep, all the same root cause -- a structure that SHRINKS or a length that is DECLARED is trusted without a bound, and the unchecked path is only reachable after a rewind or a corrupt payload. (1) A rewind that drops tile slots left the reclaim queue holding indices the tile table no longer carried, and releaseTile's `while (table[slot] !== t + 1)` walked the whole table forever: four tile slots grown from one ignition and rewound to zero leave the pre-fix kernel alive after 65 s with no output, 0.0 ms after the fix (regression section 6 of scripts/fire-tile-reclaim-witness.mjs drives the bare kernel, because defineFire's own rewind path never reached it). Fixed three ways: filter queued indices past tileCount whenever tileCount shrinks, refuse an index at or past tileCount in tryReclaim, bound the probe and throw. (2) restore() of a smaller tileCount left hashOfCell stale above the new height, so checksum() disagreed with a full rebuild -- clear the dropped range. (3) decodeFireKeyframe trusted the lengths it read: 2003 of 28047 tampered keyframes died with "Invalid typed array length" or "Offset is outside the bounds of the DataView" instead of being rejected. Every read is bounds-checked now, and cell code, fuel class, tile face, cell state and fuel-vs-class are validated; the same sweep is 22173 clean rejections, 5874 accepts, 0 crashes.
