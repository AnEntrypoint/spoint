project/mapspinner-webgpu-composite-flip-and-double-tonemap: vdrs-composite.js copies mapspinner's offscreen texture into three's linear rgba16float target. WebGPU texture v=0 is the top so the fullscreen-triangle uv must be (x*0.5+0.5, 0.5-y*0.5) (GL convention flipped terrain, sky and depth), and the display-referred pow(x,1/2.2) output must be decoded (sRGB) and have three's ACES/Reinhard tone mapping inverted (displayToTarget transfer built from renderer.toneMapping/exposure) or the target encodes it twice and the ground goes white. Verified pixel-exact (max diff 0) on NVIDIA RTX 3060 and AMD Radeon adapters.
