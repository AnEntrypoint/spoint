project/underwater-tint-shared-sea-uniform: client/core/UnderwaterTint.js drives the sea level through one shared Float32Array(3) uniform, spointSea (seaY, 1/(2R) curvature, enabled flag). It is registered on UniformsLib.fog and every ShaderLib entry, and setSeaLevelY writes it in place (commit 3a0b03daf6). It must never re-splice chunk text: three's program cache ignores chunk text, and live that left 8 of 9 materials on SPOINT_SEA_Y=-100000 (see project/three-shaderchunk-edit-needsupdate-noop). A zero uniform, e.g. on a ShaderMaterial built before install, means disabled. Witness method: gl.getShaderSource over renderer.info.programs, plus the underwater player pixel after __tpOverride to y=-25.
