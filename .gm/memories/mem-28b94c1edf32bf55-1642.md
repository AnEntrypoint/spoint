relocation.js dirToLocalXZ solves for the chart-local (x,z) whose ground point lies on the radial ray through a direction by fixed-point iteration x=(radius+elevation)*dot(d,east), z=(radius+elevation)*dot(d,north), stopping when the step drops under ELEVATION_REFINE_TOL_M=1e-3 and keeping the best ray miss. That identity is exact (|P| = radius + elevation, so x = |P|*dot(d,east)); the iteration is a CONTRACTION ONLY while the terrain's slope along the radial direction is under 1. Where the radial slope exceeds 1 the map is expansive and the iteration settles into a two-cycle instead: measured on tps-game (R=63600, seed 1337) at 28 deg from the anchor it oscillates between elevation -267.094 and -224.554, a 19.96 m horizontal jump, while the true sampler.heightAt(dir) = -233.730 lies BETWEEN the two branches - the radial ray genuinely meets the surface twice there. bestMiss then returns the initial guess, 7.4 m off the requested ray, which on 65 deg terrain reads as a 14-23 m elevation error. Extra passes do not help (identical at 8, 16, 32, 64, 200 passes) because it has converged to the cycle, not to the root. Measured frequency: 3 cells of 15360 on a 320 m/16x16 grid across 5 chart angles, 30 of 46336 across the full circumnavigation; worst 22.769 m. Consequence today: minimap relief shading differs by at most 7/255 at those cells (biome RGB is byte-identical, land flips 0), and teleport by lat/lon can land a few metres off on a cliff. A fix is a bracketed root find on g(e)=elevationAt(x(e),z(e))-e, not more passes - and it costs a solveSurfaceY per evaluation, so it does not belong in the per-cell minimap path.
