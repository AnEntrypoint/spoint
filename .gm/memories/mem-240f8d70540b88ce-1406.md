project/tsl-sky-legacy-8step-march-vs-undersampled-scattering-lut-2026-10-05: TSL sky red gap root cause (hills pose tod 0.45, legacy 56,176,236 vs TSL 92,180,238). Legacy GL sky program never receives uUseScatteringLUT (default 0) so it integrates inscatter with an 8-step midpoint march over the transmittance LUT with the horizon soft term; sky-tsl.js used the single-tap baked scattering LUT (as does WGSL sky-render.js). A Node CPU port of both reproduced the earlier dusk witness (legacy 162,225,242 vs CPU 162,225,241; TSL 167,226,244 vs CPU 164,226,242). Radiance is the first diverging stage; tint, exposure, ACES, saturation and gamma are identical code. Cause inside the LUT: the scattering LUT v axis is linear in distance-to-top with 32 rows, so at 5.8 atm-km altitude every ray steeper than ~55 deg shares row 0; LUT inscatter is 1.30-1.33x a 256-step truth there (0.99 below 50 deg), giving +25..30 R at zenith. The 8-step legacy is within ~5% of truth at zenith and up to ~11 levels off at 60 deg. Fix (bdc4409d47): createSkyTSL ports the legacy 8-step march (marchSteps) for sky and ground; marchRadiance (LUT) stays for terrain aerial perspective. CPU: half-float LUT quantization moves output <=0.08 of an 8-bit level over 50 poses x 5 sun elevations. Predicted post-fix sky crop mean = legacy within ~2 levels per channel, meanAbs <= 3.
