project/planetframe-exact-render-inverse: the planet is drawn under the rigid map localToWorld(x,y,z)=up(R+aH+y)+e x+n z (TerrainBackdrop eye), water at radius R, ground at R+h(dir). src/terrain/PlanetFrame.js is the ONE convention: waterlineLocalY = true sphere (sphereSurfaceLocalY elev 0), elevationAtLocal = |localToWorld|-R, localToDir = sea-level orthographic dir (optional y), solveSurfaceY = stateless secant+bisection solve of |localToWorld|=R+h(dir), stop when the sample moves < R*2^-24 horizontally, max 8 evals (tps-game: 1.92 evals <=300 m, 2.88 to 7 km, residual <= 9.2 mm; diverges only when slope*r/R >= 1, e.g. R=1000 with reliefScale 0.001). patch-baker heightFn, bake-heightfield, terrain.glsl sculptOverrideAt(dir0,hBase) x=(R+hBase)*dot(dir0,east) all use it. The old gnomonic localToDir put physics terrain 23.5 m off the rendered shoreline at 6 km (0.62 m at 2 km). localToDir must still return a FRESH array (ClimateCache/patch-baker dirToFace keep it); east=cross(ref,up). Witness method: depth-occluded Lambert-emissive pole + readPixels column, and W.probeRendered iterating render.sampleGroundMSync through the exact inverse (commit 7c73d8d16c).
