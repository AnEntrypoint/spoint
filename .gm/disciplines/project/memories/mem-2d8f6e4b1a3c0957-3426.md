Deep-detail tier for AGENTS.md "Netcode and wire". AGENTS.md keeps the rule, the constant and the script; this memo keeps the measurements and the failure scenarios.

Drift, prediction, separation
- Replay/lockstep drift comes from dt, not Jolt: 1e-6 relative dt error is 0.75 mm/tick. Client-side prediction runs no collision, so `resimulate()` walks the local character into geometry it is held against; the fix is a wedge flag gating the resim step when held.
- Client peer separation is a win: `CollisionSystem.js applyPlayerCollisions` pushes overlap/2 per tick. Measured at 0/0/0: 0.1943 and 0.2049 corrections/ack with it on vs 0.7422 control — near 0.74 means push-OFF regardless of the flag.

Wire
- `msgpack.js` `WIRE_STRUCTURES[1]` must list exactly the keys, in order, of `TickHandler.js _packPayload`: seq,tick,serverTime,players,entities,removed,delta,dots. msgpackr 2.0.4 writes -0 as the single byte `0x00`, so every peer decoded +0; `patch-deps.mjs` (postinstall) excludes -0 from both integer tests and runs strict.
- Wire v3 player record: 8 fields in a 22-byte bin. Recipient-only `[inputSequence, inputBuffer, groundNormal]` ride the per-recipient `me` block, so snapshots pack per recipient.
- Raw ws clients (`perf-gate.mjs`, `verify-session.mjs`) mirror `COALESCE_SENTINEL`/`splitCoalesced`: `0xFF` then `[uint32 LE length][msgpack]` repeated; a plain `unpack()` of a coalesced frame throws.
- `getSnapshot()` is keyed on `_version === _snapshotVersion` alone (the tick is gone); a per-runtime map reuses an entity's encoding when a field-by-field compare finds no change.

Snapshot planning and interest
- `SnapshotGroupPlanner`: groups = `ceil(viewers*perViewerMs/(budgetMs-fixedMs))`, budget = tick period * `SNAP_COST_HIGH_FRAC` 0.35 (`TickHandler.js:30`), medians over the last 32 non-keyframe passes.
- Interest management is off by default: `relevanceRadius || 0` (`server.js:67`) makes `playersInInterest` return every player and no world sets it; the 3.6-6.7 KB/s in `docs/netcode.md` is that harness, not scaling.
- Lockstep: rollback is input-capped, measure by depth not rate (`maxRollbackTicks` 12). `inputDelayTicks` above `INPUT_RETAIN_TICKS` (240) throws at construction. Any new per-tick gameplay timer in `AppRuntime` is tick-indexed, never `Date.now()` (`_interactCooldowns` stores `runtime.currentTick + ticks`); a wall-clock expiry desyncs under resim.
- Authoritative path: prediction is off by default (`app.js predictionEnabled`, only `?predict`); `INPUT_BACKLOG_DRAIN` merges <=2 queued inputs into the last (drops movement); rollback/lockstep are unwired.

Session and transport churn (why each rule exists)
- A socket becomes a player by MIGRATE, by RECONNECT, or by staying silent past the MIGRATE-peek grace timer (`ServerHandlers.js onClientConnect`, 50 ms, widened to 1500 ms by tick dilation). Any socket reaching `/ws` and sending no first message is therefore spawned as a phantom: measured as an extra player id 2 sitting at spawn in a 2-client arena room, courtesy of `ServerBrowser.js pingWs` re-pinging `/ws` every 15 s. Probes use `?probe=1`: `ServerAPI.js attachWSHandlers` reads the query off the upgrade request, marks the transport `probeOnly`, and `onClientConnect` returns before the peek timer, closing after 10 s. The grace timer also skips a transport whose close already landed.
- Answering an unresolvable RECONNECT with INVALID_SESSION while leaving the socket open stranded the client: connected, no player, never retried, while its pre-spawned player sat in the room as a phantom. `ServerHandlers rejectSession` now flushes the reason and closes; `PhysicsNetworkClient._dropSessionAndReconnect` drops the token and reconnects. A refused MIGRATE closes the candidate without spawning.
- An acked migration the client then abandons (main thread blocked past `_confirmMigration`'s 4 s, or a dead WebTransport candidate) destroyed the player and orphaned the original socket for good — the page kept playing with no server-side player and a two-player room never formed. `_handleMigrate` keeps the original transport as a fallback owner and hands the player back when the candidate dies; `ConnectionManager` ignores a close from a transport its client no longer owns. Arm F measured 6 -> 5 players with 0 further snapshots before the fix, 6 -> 6 and 18 -> 63 after.
- `scripts/transport-churn-witness.mjs` covers arms A-E plus F (abandoned candidate), 21 checks, RESULT: PASS.

Prediction lead clamp (measured 2026-10-07)
- `src/client/BaseClient.js sendInput` skips `predEngine.addInput` once `predEngine.predictionLeadSteps()` (`_inputSeq - 1 - _lastAckedSeq`, 0 before the first server state) reaches `MAX_PREDICTION_LEAD_STEPS` 16, and still retransmits the unacked window every tick, so a fast client degrades to a bounded lead instead of an unbounded one.
- Forcing the engagement: `--server-tick=<hz>` on `scripts/prediction-drift-witness.mjs` calls `server.tickSystem.setTickRate()` after boot, so the loop runs at that rate while the handshake keeps advertising the world's 60 and the client keeps sending. It is the only lever that pushes the lead past 16 — snapshots are floored at `SNAP_RATE_MIN_HZ` and the client's own rate is pinned to the advertised one (`INPUT_RATE_MAX_ADJUST` 0.04), so a healthy server never gets there.
- Clamp ON, `--gpu=nvidia --server-tick=10 --hold-ms=8000 --settle-ms=6000 --divergence-cap=6 --settle-tol=0.8`: unacked pinned 13-16 (peak 16), peak divergence 2.907 m, inputsSent 39.6/s, serverTps 10.0, serverAppliedFraction 0.986, RESULT: PASS.
- Clamp OFF (injected `MAX_PREDICTION_LEAD_STEPS = Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY`), same arm: unacked 127-152, divergence 5.483 -> 17.267 m, clientTravel 32.550 m vs serverTravel 16.800 m (applied fraction 0.516), RESULT: FAIL. Bounding the lead also roughly doubles what the server applies: a deep backlog starves it.
- That arm needs raised caps: 16 steps of travel against a 10 Hz server is ~3 m, and the residual floor is one 100 ms step (0.35-0.53 m), not the 3 m / 0.5 m defaults a 60 Hz server earns.
